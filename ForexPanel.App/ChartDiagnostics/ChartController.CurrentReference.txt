Important observed behavior:
- Candlestick chart with 250 generated candles.
- Right-side price axis and bottom DateTime axis.
- Left mouse drag in the plot body pans both X and Y.
- Dragging the rightmost 100 px changes vertical price scale.
- Dragging the bottom 35 px sets timeScaleDrag, but PreviewMouseMove does not process timeScaleDrag.
- Mouse wheel shifts the X range by 10%; it does not zoom.
- Built-in ScottPlot left-drag pan and right-drag zoom are disabled.
- Built-in ScottPlot mouse-wheel zoom is removed.

Diagnostic conclusion from the implementation:
The current chart body uses custom mouse interaction logic instead of ScottPlot 5's standard interaction responses. Horizontal time zoom is not implemented, bottom-axis drag is incomplete, and wheel behavior is scroll-only.
